Projects Delivered

Communication Shift: a strategic change from emotional, top-funnel messaging to a functional, benefit-oriented communication based on audience awareness insights, contributing to performance growth alongside sales and marketing initiatives.

BDAY: V4 Company’s birthday campaign. A commercial initiative offering special conditions, aggressive discounts, and new product packages as a hook for acquiring new clients. Launched starting in September, it was identified as the driver behind sustaining page conversion rate even as investment increased month over month (from R$99,599 in May to R$177,279 in October).
