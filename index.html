About

Writer since 2017, Copywriter since 2021.

Every single company I worked with has benefited from a primary skill of mine: my ability to deep learning and deep work.

Alongside with those skills, as a self-taught student, I explored multiple topics to enhance value on a professional standpoint: marketing, entrepreneurship, business, branding, positioning, statistics, data and analytics…

Early Life

I was born on March 14th, 2002, in Indaiatuba, São Paulo, Brazil. Always encouraged by my parents to pursue education so that I could provide for myself in a way that they couldn’t.

At 15, I remember writing my first essay for a Portuguese class and immediately became captivated by it.

In those years I used writing as a way to express myself, and later on as a way to earn my first financial income.
